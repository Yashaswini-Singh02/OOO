Stack and decisions

- Everything must work on live input. OO_FIXTURES=1 (default) uses fixtures/; OO_FIXTURES=0 uses live providers.
- Live providers: Google Places (places, shops, details, photos), YouTube, Tavily (Instagram links, events), Open-Meteo. Daily caps in app/quota.py: Tavily 30, Google 200, YouTube 50.
- Chat channel: Telegram (@outof_office_bot, long polling, no tunnel). WhatsApp code exists but Meta locked the business account (error 131031).
- cfo.ai is for the business plan only. Personal budgets run in our code.

What is built

- Graph (app/pipeline.py): trigger → scout → taste → budget → wardrobe → planner → verifier → feed. A failed gate loops back to scout, at most two replans, then silence.
- Verifier: refuses venues that are missing, closed, or misplaced by a reel; checks day plans for time conflicts; checks a shop's craft against data/gi_crafts.json.
- Goa conflict: Dudhsagar trek and a west-coast sunset can't share a day, so they are split across two days with outfits.
- Runtime (app/runtime.py): on_location() takes one location update and returns at most one nudge. Triggers: near saved spot, place just opened, rain starting, touch grass, speciality. Throttle 30 minutes, no repeat venue in 24 hours.
- Simulator: python -m app.sim (Goa walk) and python -m app.sim route_routine.
- Chat layer (app/assistant.py, app/messages.py, app/telegram.py), with a SQLite file per traveller in data/users/ (gitignored):
  - location pin → one verified suggestion card with photo, description, "Reviews say", facts, reason and buttons
  - live location → proactive nudges through the runtime
  - trip plan for a named place ("plan 2 days in Udaipur") with day themes, hooks, a hidden gem or crowd favourite with reel, and tap-to-open places
  - one-time requests ("somewhere to eat"), lasting likes and dislikes, "Suggest another", "Tell me more", "More videos", save, rate
  - budgets: set, spend, status
  - packing by text or outfit photo, outfits matched to photo spots, "what do you remember about me", /forget
- Videos and reels must pass two checks: they name the place, and a model judges the place to be the subject.
